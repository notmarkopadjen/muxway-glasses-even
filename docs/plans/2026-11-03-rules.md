# Feature: a rule under the title and one above the hint

> Status: proposed. The owner's wish of 2026-11-03, muxway-glasses-even#46;
> on top of the banner fix (#41, `2026-11-03-pages-above-the-banner.md`).

## 1. Goal

The pane view is a title row and eight lines of the pane, the hint
under them, nothing between: the owner finds it hard to follow where
the title ends and the pane begins, and where the pane ends. He wants a
horizontal line under the title and one above the hint, and not at the
price of body lines.

There is no spare pixel: the title is one 28 px line and the eight body
lines end at the banner's top. A sampler build on the device on
2026-11-03 showed two things the simulator cannot: a bordered text
container two pixels high draws as a rule, and a title row 26 px high
shows its text whole. The owner chose this over a boxed title and over
seven lines in a box:

```
◐ glasses-release · Claude                          following   ← 0–26
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────   ← 26–28
line 1 of the pane                                               ← 28–252,
…                                                                  eight lines
line 8 of the pane
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────   ← 253–255
▽ page · ● answer · ≡ menu · ×× back                            ← 258–288
```

The lower rule sits in the six pixels a banner takes, so it is left out
while a toast is up; nothing else moves. The columns get the same: their
titles at 26 px, one rule across under them, the bodies as they are,
the lower rule under them. Three columns are three titles, three
bodies, the hint and one rule, the page's eight containers, so with
three columns only the upper rule is drawn; with two, both.

The owner, seeing the pane on the device, asked for the same line on
every screen with a footer. So every page gets the lower rule: the
answers, the confirmation and the inbox (OS lists, whose container ends
at 240), the suggestion, the listening, transcribing and failed pages
and the heard page (boxes, which end well above). The pane list has no
container to spare (seven rows and the hint are the page's eight), so
there the hint is boxed without padding: its own top edge is the line,
at the hint's top rather than in the gap, and the banner replaces it as
before. The upper rule stays where there is a title over a body: the
pane and the columns.

## 2. Design

`src/glasses.ts`: a block may carry `pad`, the border's padding when it
is not the default; the rule is a bordered block two pixels high, full
width, with no padding and no text. `RULE_H = 2`.

`src/views.ts`: `TITLE_H` becomes `LINE_H - RULE_H` (26), `BODY_Y`
stays 28 (the title and the rule), so `BODY_LINES` and `BOX_ROWS` are
unchanged; a `rule(name, y)` helper; `panePage` pushes the upper rule
after the title and, when `m.toast` is null, the lower one at
`BANNER_Y + 1`; `splitPage` the same, the lower one only with two
columns. The geometry test from #41 holds: every block ends at or
above the banner, the lower rule included (255 < 252 is false, so it
is checked against the hint's top, `HINT_Y`, with the banner absent).

A toast appearing or leaving changes the page's geometry, which is a
rebuild already (`hintBorder` is part of it), so the lower rule comes
and goes with the banner without a new rule for upgrades.
